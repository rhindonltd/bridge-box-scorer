# Room Display Guide

The **Room Display** puts the session **timer** or the live **leaderboard** on
a shared screen so the whole room can see it — for example a TV or projector at
the front of the playing area.

Anyone can open a display; it is read-only and does not need director access.

---

## Opening a display

1. From the home screen, tap **Room Display**.
2. Choose the game from the list (**Display Game**).
3. Choose what to show:
   - **Timer** — the round countdown.
   - **Leaderboard** — live standings.

---

## The timer display

Each section has its own timer. If the game has more than one section, the
timer display first asks you to **choose a section**; the countdown then shows
that section's timer. A **← Sections** button lets you switch to a different
section. Single-section games skip this step and show the timer straight away.

A full-screen countdown designed to be read from across the room:

- A very large **MM:SS** countdown on a black background.
- The **title** and, during play, the current **board(s)** label.
- The clock turns **red and pulses** in the final seconds of a play phase (the
  warning period the director set).
- During the **changeover** between rounds ("move" phase) the clock is shown
  in a different colour.
- During a **break** it shows a tea-cup icon and **"Next round starts at …"**.
- **PAUSED** appears if the director has paused the timer.
- The **projected end time** of the session is shown at the bottom.

Every device shows the same time, so the display and players' screens stay in
step.

---

## The leaderboard display

For a **matchpoint pairs** game the leaderboard first asks how to show the
standings — as a **Percentage** or as **Matchpoints** — since matchpoints can be
read either way. Pick one and the board opens showing that. Other games have a
single natural presentation and go straight to the board.

A full-screen board of live standings, sized to be read from across the room
and updating as results come in:

- For **pairs** games, rankings by the game's scoring method
  (matchpoints/percentage, IMPs, or Cross-IMPs).
- For a **Swiss Pairs** game, Victory Points with a column per round and each
  pair's running total.
- For a **Swiss Teams** game, Victory Points with a column per round and each
  team's running total.
- For **teams** games, the team match / overall standings.
- For **multi-section** events, each section's standings plus a combined
  overall ranking across all sections.

The board is designed to run hands-free on the screen:

- The heading shows the **event name** (and the **section** when a single
  section is on screen).
- When there are lots of pairs and the screen is wide, the standings fill it by
  **spreading into two columns** so more places show at once.
- For a **multi-section** event it **cycles automatically** — combined, then
  each section in turn, about a minute each. Tapping a **section tab** jumps
  straight to that view and restarts the cycle.
- If a list is still too long to fit, it **scrolls slowly** to the bottom and
  back so every place comes into view. The column headings stay in place at the
  top while the places scroll underneath, so it's always clear what each column
  shows.

Standings update automatically whenever a result is confirmed or a director
correction is made — there is nothing to refresh.

---

## Tips

- Put the **timer** on the main screen during play and switch to the
  **leaderboard** at the end (or use two screens/devices, one for each).
